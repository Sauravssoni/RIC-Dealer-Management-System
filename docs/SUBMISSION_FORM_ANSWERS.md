# RAJ-AGRIPAY - Submission Form Answers

All answers are intentionally under 500 characters and aligned to the Integrated Dealer Management System challenge.

## Description of solution and its fit to the proposed challenge
RAJ-AGRIPAY is Rajasthan Agriculture's dealer claim-to-settlement control plane. It unifies dealer lifecycle, scheme rules, invoice evidence, approval, finance routing and reconciliation while retaining RajKisan, IFMS 3.0, PFMS/SNA-SPARSH and Treasury as authoritative systems. It directly targets T+15-T+30 delays, fragmented ledgers, manual verification and poor payment visibility.

## Challenge - technology, problem addressed and proposed solution
Dealer payments are delayed by fragmented scheme systems, repeated identity checks, manual invoice scrutiny, disconnected approvals and weak claim-to-payment lineage. RAJ-AGRIPAY creates one Dealer Passport and evidence-backed Claim Truth Graph, applies versioned scheme rules, routes finance-ready packets through Rajasthan's existing rails and reconciles every claim to authoritative payment references.

## Value proposition
Syntheon offers a low-cost, API-first Dealer Payment Orchestration Fabric: federated Dealer Passport, GST e-invoice/QR-first verification with OCR fallback, low-code Scheme Rule Studio, explainable risk checks, human-authority approvals, IFMS/PFMS routing, claim-to-UTR reconciliation, dealer alerts, audit replay, BHASHINI assistance and optional SUTRA-ID Edge for assisted/offline capture.

## How the technology addresses the challenge
We reuse Rajasthan's existing digital infrastructure instead of duplicating it: RajKisan for Agriculture dealer truth, Raj Sewa Dwaar for approved API integration, IFMS 3.0/PFMS/SNA-SPARSH for sovereign payment rails and GST structured evidence where authorised. A 90-day pilot measures clean-claim validation, departmental approval, duplicate detection, reconciliation, audit retrieval and exception reduction across contrasting schemes/districts.

## Available resource / competitive advantage / USP
Most solutions stop at upload, OCR, approve and PFMS. RAJ-AGRIPAY adds the missing Agriculture intelligence before and after Finance: scheme-aware Claim Truth Graph, effective-dated rules, cross-scheme duplicate controls, Payment Identity Lock, explicit integration-truth labels and end-to-end audit lineage. SUTRA-ID Edge/BHASHINI add proven assisted access without making hardware mandatory or permitting offline public-expenditure approval.

## Estimated project development and implementation cost
INR 44.8 lakh indicative 90-day pilot, subject to confirmed Government interface scope, production hosting/security requirements and taxes. The cost is kept low by reusing RajKisan, Raj Sewa Dwaar, IFMS 3.0, PFMS/SNA-SPARSH, GST and other authorised Government infrastructure rather than building parallel payment, identity or proprietary low-code platforms.
