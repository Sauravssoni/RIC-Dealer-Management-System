# Portal-Ready Form Answers - Integrated Dealer Management System

> The copied application UI currently shows an AgriStack description heading, but these answers are for the **Integrated Dealer Management System - Dealers' Billing, Payments, Onboarding & Renewals** challenge. Verify the challenge title immediately before submission.

## Challenge
RAJ-AGRIPAY is Rajasthan Agriculture's dealer claim-to-settlement control plane. It addresses T+15-T+30 delays, paper invoice checks, fragmented scheme ledgers and poor dealer/auditor visibility without duplicating RajKisan or IFMS. A federated Dealer Passport, IRN/QR-first invoice intelligence with OCR fallback, deterministic Scheme Packs, Claim Truth checks, human approval, IFMS/PFMS routing, alerts and claim-to-UTR reconciliation create one auditable digital journey.

## Value proposition
Syntheon offers a low-cost, API-first Dealer Lifecycle & Payment Evidence Fabric: onboarding/renewal, licence/payee references, invoice capture, explainable validation, maker-checker approvals, exception ownership, Finance handoff, dealer status, district MIS and reconciliation. Existing Government rails remain authoritative, reducing integration risk and lock-in. New schemes are effective-dated configuration over reusable services rather than new software projects.

## How the technology addresses the challenge
The 90-day pilot maps current schemes and baselines, then activates Dealer Passport, structured e-invoice/OCR intake, rule packs, duplicate/rate/quantity controls, officer work queues, State/CSS route resolution, notifications and reconciliation. Raj Sewa Dwaar is the intended integration path; Raj eSign/e-Sanchar are Rajasthan-native signing/messaging adapters; IFMS/PFMS/Treasury remain settlement authorities. BHASHINI/SUTRA add optional multilingual assisted/offline capture. Targets include <4h clean validation, <=T+2 departmental approval and >=90% eligible auto-reconciliation.

## Available resource / USP
Most alternatives stop at dealer login + OCR + dashboard + PFMS. RAJ-AGRIPAY starts where Rajasthan's existing systems stop: Agriculture-specific claim truth. It prefers machine-readable GST evidence to OCR, blocks exact duplicates with reason codes, locks risky bank-profile changes, replays claims against the correct historical Scheme Pack and preserves Claim->approval->acknowledgement->UTR evidence. Syntheon's governed-AI, ledger and multilingual edge experience enables a functional officer dashboard plus optional SUTRA last-mile workflow without allowing AI/device autonomy over public expenditure.
