## Challenge

## Decision requested
Approve a 90-day Rajasthan Agriculture pilot of RAJ-AGRIPAY at an indicative implementation ask of **INR 44.8 lakh**, subject to confirmed Government interfaces, hosting/security scope and taxes.

## Product thesis
**IFMS moves public money. RAJ-AGRIPAY proves why an Agriculture dealer claim is ready to move, routes it correctly, and closes the evidence loop after it moves.**

## What the evaluator can test
- `/dashboard` officer operations, claim/payment ledger, SLA, exception control, district MIS and integrations.
- `/onboarding` dealer onboarding and renewal / Dealer Passport.
- `/intake` IRN/QR-first invoice extraction, OCR fallback, confidence review and deterministic Claim Truth.
- `/impact` editable economic scenario model.
- Dashboard alerts designed for Rajasthan e-Sanchar 3.0.
- SUTRA assisted/offline evidence capture with online-only expenditure authority.

## Core controls
Dealer Passport · Payment Identity Lock · structured invoice first · Claim Truth Graph · versioned Scheme Packs · maker-checker · Raj eSign-ready approval · IFMS/PFMS routing · claim-to-UTR reconciliation · e-Sanchar-ready alerts · append-only evidence lineage.

## Statewide vision
Phase 1 dealer settlement → Phase 2 Agriculture Payment Evidence Fabric → Phase 3 future FarmGraph AI interoperability for demand forecasting, access-gap detection and scheme/extension planning → Phase 4 reusable Public Expenditure Evidence Engine.

Future stages are not represented as current/live integrations.
